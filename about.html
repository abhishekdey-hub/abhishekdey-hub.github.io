About Me

Building inclusive digital experiences and accessible technology.

Who I Am

Hello! I am Abhishek Dey, a dedicated web developer with a deep passion for building accessible technology. My primary mission is to create digital solutions, web directories, and mobile application hubs that empower the visually impaired community.

I believe that technology truly shines when it is usable by everyone without barriers. Whether curating screen-reader-friendly games on VisionPlay or organizing essential utilities in my App Store, accessibility and simplicity always come first.

Core Focus

Designing clean, semantic, and screen-reader-compatible interfaces using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Open Source & Community

Sharing curated tools, documentation, and accessible resources openly on GitHub for everyone to benefit from.
